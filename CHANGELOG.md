# CamBatch Changelog

## 1.0.0

- First public release.
- Ordered, persistent multi-camera render list.
- Final and Viewport batch rendering.
- Progressive Cycles Render Result window.
- Single-camera test rendering.
- Native output, image-format, transparency, and resolution controls.
- Existing-file detection and overwrite confirmation.
- Blender 4.5 LTS and 5.x support.
